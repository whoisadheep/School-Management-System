# Financial Ledger, Expenses & Financial Dashboard

> **Module**: Accounting & Financial Control  
> **Sidebar Tabs**:  
> - **Financial Dashboard** `[NAV:dashboard]`  
> - **Expenses & Ledger** `[NAV:expenses]`  
> **Access**: Super Admin, Admin, Accountant

---

## 1. Financial Dashboard `[NAV:dashboard]`

The Financial Dashboard provides the principal and school bursar with real-time institutional metrics:
- **Total Revenue**: Cumulative fees collected for the current academic session.
- **Total Operational Expenses**: Money spent across electricity, maintenance, salaries, supplies, etc.
- **Net Operating Surplus / Balance**: Total Revenue minus Total Expenses.
- **Outstanding Fee Dues**: Total unpaid fee balances across all enrolled students.
- **Monthly Revenue vs Expense Charts**: Visual trend graphs comparing monthly collections against expenditures.

## 2. Recording School Expenses

The **Expenses & Ledger** module records all operational costs outside of student billing.

### Step-by-Step: Recording an Expense
1. Go to **Expenses & Ledger** from the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Record Expense**.
3. Enter Expense Details:
   - **Expense Category**: Select from Utilities (Electricity, Water, Internet), Building Maintenance, Laboratory Consumables, Sports Equipment, Office Supplies, Staff Welfare, Event Hosting, etc.
   - **Amount Paid**: Dollar or local currency value.
   - **Date of Expense**: Defaults to today.
   - **Paid To (Vendor/Payee)**: Name of contractor, vendor, or service provider.
   - **Payment Method**: Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Corporate Card.
   - **Reference / Invoice No**: Vendor bill or receipt number.
   - **Description / Notes**: Specific reason (e.g., "Annual servicing of Campus Generator").
4. Click **Save Expense**. The expense is immediately debited to the school ledger.

## 3. General Ledger & School Daybook

The General Ledger offers a double-entry style chronological stream of every rupee/dollar moving in and out of the school:
- **Credits (Green)**: Fee collections, donation receipts, library fines, hostel dues.
- **Debits (Red)**: Operational expenses, staff salary disbursements, vendor settlements.
- **Running School Balance**: Real-time cash-on-hand and bank balance totals.

### Filtering and Exporting the Ledger
- **Date Range Filters**: View Daybook for Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom Date Range.
- **Category Filters**: Filter transactions by specific expense heads or fee streams.
- **Exporting**: Click **Export PDF Statement** or **Export to Excel / CSV** for external auditors and tax compliance.
