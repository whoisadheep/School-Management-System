# Staff Management & Monthly Payroll

> **Module**: Human Resources & Payroll  
> **Sidebar Tab**: **Staff Directory**  
> **Deep Link**: `[NAV:staff]`  
> **Access**: Super Admin, Admin

---

## 1. Staff Directory

The Staff Directory centralizes records for teachers, administrative personnel, support staff, and school drivers.

### How to Add a New Staff Member
1. Go to **Staff Directory** from the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Staff Member**.
3. Fill in staff details:
   - **Personal Info**: Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Contact Phone, Email, Address.
   - **Employment Info**: Employee ID (e.g., `EMP-104`), Designation (e.g., *Senior Mathematics Teacher*, *Accountant*, *Bus Driver*), Department (e.g., *Science*, *Administration*), Date of Joining.
   - **Qualifications & Experience**: Highest degree, previous experience, emergency contact.
4. Upload staff photo and relevant documents (resume, identity proof, certificates).
5. Click **Save Staff Member**.

## 2. Salary Structures & Monthly Payroll

Eduvia includes a structured payroll module to calculate gross pay, allowances, deductions, and net salaries.

### Step 1: Configure Staff Salary Components
1. In **Staff Directory**, click on a staff member and open their **Salary & Payroll** tab.
2. Enter the salary components:
   - **Base Salary / Basic Pay**: Fixed monthly base.
   - **Allowances**: House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance, Medical Allowance, Special Bonus.
   - **Deductions**: Provident Fund (PF), Income Tax, Professional Tax, Health Insurance deductions.
3. The system calculates the net monthly take-home salary.
4. Click **Save Salary Structure**.

### Step 2: Generate Monthly Pay Slips
1. At the end of the month, open the **Payroll Management** section.
2. Select the target Month and Year.
3. Review auto-calculated net pay (adjusted for unpaid leaves if applicable).
4. Click **Generate Pay Slip** (or **Generate All Pay Slips** for bulk payroll).
5. Click **Print Pay Slip** to produce a professional PDF slip featuring:
   - School header and logo.
   - Employee ID, designation, and PAN/Tax ID.
   - Itemized table of earnings and deductions.
   - Net pay in figures and words, with signature fields for Accounts and Employee.

## 3. Teacher Attendance & Leaves

- **Daily Check-In**: Record daily teacher attendance under the **Attendance** module [NAV:attendance] $\rightarrow$ **Teacher Attendance** tab.
- **Leave Recording**: Mark staff on Sick Leave, Casual Leave, or Maternity Leave. Leave counts can be linked to payroll deductions.

---

## 4. FAQs

- **Q: Can a teacher see other teachers' salaries?**  
  *A:* No. Teacher user accounts only have access to academic modules (Students, Attendance, Exams). Only Super Admins and Admins can access the Staff and Payroll tabs.
